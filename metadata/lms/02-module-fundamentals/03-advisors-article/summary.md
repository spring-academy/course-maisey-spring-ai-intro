You now understand the Advisors API of Spring AI, an interceptor that wraps a ChatClient call to act before the request reaches the model and after the response returns. Advisors form a chain, share a context map, and let you add cross-cutting features such as logging, memory, and retrieval as a configuration change on the ChatClient. You also saw how `getOrder()` decides where an advisor sits in the chain, with `HIGHEST_PRECEDENCE` and `LOWEST_PRECEDENCE` as the two extremes, and how a recursive advisor loops the chain for patterns such as tool calling and output validation.

In the next section you put this into practice in a hands-on lab, where you first build your own logging advisor step by step and then swap it for the built-in `SimpleLoggerAdvisor`.
