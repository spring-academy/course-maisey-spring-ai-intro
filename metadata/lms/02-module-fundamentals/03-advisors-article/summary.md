You now understand Spring AI's advisor concept, an interceptor that wraps a ChatClient call to act before the request reaches the model and after the response returns. Advisors form a chain, share a context map, and let you add cross-cutting features like logging, memory, and retrieval as a configuration change on the ChatClient. You also saw how getOrder controls where an advisor sits in the chain, with HIGHEST_PRECEDENCE and LOWEST_PRECEDENCE for the extremes, and how recursive advisors loop the chain for patterns like tool calling and output validation.
